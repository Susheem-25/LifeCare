Problem Context

WHY LifeCare: The Indian Emergency Crisis

Over 50% of trauma fatalities in India occur due to transit delays and hospital gate admission bottlenecks.

01

Panic & Language Barriers

Calling 108 in moments of acute panic causes confusion, language mismatches, and inaccurate symptom narration, losing up to 8 critical minutes before vehicle dispatch.

02

"Blind Driving" & Rejection

40%+ of ambulances rush blindly to hospitals only to find zero available ICU beds, missing specialist doctors, or non-functional life-support equipment.

03

20-35 Min Paper Delays

While patients suffer myocardial infarction or trauma shock, relatives are trapped filling physical paper registration forms and consent sheets at the ER counter.

Execution Flow

HOW It Works: The 4-Stage Zero-Wait Mission

Deterministic progression from symptom onset to resuscitation bay intake.

STAGE 1

Speech-to-Triage

0.4s Latency

Bystander speaks in Hindi or English. Sarvam AI maps symptoms into ICD-10 codes and ESI Level 1-5 urgency.

STAGE 2

Atomic Bed Lock

<10ms Lock

Simultaneously reserves an ER trauma bay, on-duty specialist doctor, and life-support machinery with zero overbooking risk.

STAGE 3

Auto 15s ER Siren

Instant Audio

The hospital ER dashboard automatically rings a loud 15-second dual-tone trauma siren across tabs, alerting staff prior to arrival.

STAGE 4

0-Second Gate Admission

Zero Paper Delay

Paramedic streams live ECG & vitals in-transit. ER camera scans the encrypted QR code, bypassing the admission counter directly.

Core Engineering

Deep-Dive: Breakthrough Features

Engineered to eliminate the exact operational failures common in Indian emergency healthcare.

Acoustic Alert Protocol

Automatic 15-Second Trauma Siren

Client-side synthesized modulated dual-tone wail (620Hz–980Hz) via Web Audio API. Dispatched through BroadcastChannel & cross-tab storage events with zero manual clicks required.

Sarvam AI Integration

Pure Bilingual Voice AI Engine

Strict linguistic routing: 100% pure Hindi audio synthesis when browsing in Hindi; 100% authoritative English medical voice when browsing in English.

Clinical Rigor

Doctor Rostering with Degrees & Gender

Doctors cataloged with verified medical credentials (MBBS, MD, DM, FRCS), explicit gender identification, and surgery duty availability status.

NIST SP 800-38D

AES-GCM-256 E2EE & Zero-PII Shield

All in-transit paperwork and vitals are sealed cryptographically with SHA-256 key fingerprints. No plain-text phone numbers or names are exposed to bystander cameras.
