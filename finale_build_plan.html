2 Where We Will Be: Target State by 12:00 PM, 22 November
Finale Commitments
1. Firebase Hosting & Google Auth

Production hosting on Firebase. Role-based Google Sign-In for Guardians, Drivers, and Hospital Staff with auto-generated unique IDs (GRD-XXXX, DRV-XXXX, HSP-XXXX) and partitioned Firestore collections.

2. Real DigiLocker E2EE Pipeline

Production DigiLocker/ABDM integration fetching ABHA records with end-to-end encryption to ER bays. Absolutely zero name, DOB, or phone revealed on our site; patients are tracked strictly via unique patient IDs.

3. Live Neo4j & Pure-Software Telemetry

Live AuraDB Bolt connection locking trauma bays and specialists in <9ms. Paramedic Cockpit streams live vitals purely via software (WebSockets/Firestore) with zero physical hardware dependencies.

3 User Segments Served
Multi-Role Architecture
Primary: Distressed Bystanders / Guardians

Panicking citizens who need a 10-second Indic voice SOS, zero-form entry, instant hospital bed lock, and direct navigation without navigating complex healthcare red tape.

Secondary: 108 Ambulance EMTs / Drivers

Mobile crews receiving turn-by-turn ER navigation, contraindicated drug allergy alerts, and a pure-software telemetry dial pad to transmit vitals in-transit on standard phones/tablets.

Tertiary: Hospital ER Teams & Specialists

Trauma bay physicians receiving pre-arrival live telemetry and pre-admitted, zero-PII ABDM dossiers to prep surgical suites before wheels touch the ambulance bay.

4 Partner Tech Choice & Product Need Solved (Sarvam AI & Neo4j)
Deepened Technologies
Sarvam AI (Indic Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech)

Product Need Solved: Eliminates the severe language, literacy, and typing barrier during emergency panic. Enables high-accuracy transcription of regional Indian accents and colloquial distress cries, automatically parsing clinical urgency and vocalizing calming first-aid instructions back in the user's native tongue.

Neo4j AuraDB (Sub-9ms Atomic Graph Resource Lock)

Product Need Solved: Eliminates fatal hospital double-booking collisions. Emergency care requires interdependent resources (Hospital → Trauma Bay → Equipment → On-Call Specialist). Neo4j's Cypher graph engine executes ACID multi-resource reservations atomically in under 9ms, ensuring deterministic capacity before ambulance arrival.

*Note on Hardware: We deliberately omit Arduino/hardware pulse sensors in favor of pure-software telemetry on existing paramedic devices, ensuring frictionless deployment across India's 25,000+ government 108 ambulances without procurement delays.
